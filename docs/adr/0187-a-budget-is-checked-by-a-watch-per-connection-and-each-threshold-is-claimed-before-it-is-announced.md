# A budget is checked by a watch per connection, and each threshold is claimed before it is announced

> **Accepted.** The types and every rule are in `src/hermes/budgets.ts` (`windowOf`, `evaluate`, `forecastOf`, `dueAlert`, `shelfOf`), the reads, storage and notifications in `src/hermes/budgetWatch.ts` (`BudgetWatch`, `BudgetWatches`, `budgetShelves`), the tab in `src/ui/Budgets.tsx`, and its place in Usage in `src/ui/UsageAnalytics.tsx`. `collectUsage` (`src/hermes/usageAnalytics.ts`) keeps finished compression segments across checks. The per-model route is the `modelUsage` capability. Tested in `src/hermes/budgets.test.ts`, `src/hermes/budgetWatch.test.ts`, `src/hermes/usageAnalytics.test.ts` and `src/ui/budgets.test.tsx`. Issue #605, first PR: limits and alerts, no brakes.

The owner's bots run on their own, and a loop left running overnight can spend money nobody sees until the bill. The owner asked to set what each server, bot, project or model may spend per day, week or month, see where spending is heading and which chats spent it, and be told at 50, 80 and 100%. Hermes enforces no money budget at any commit the app supports (`bf0203a`, `f0d9488`), and has no read of spending by when it happened or of chats sorted by cost.

## Decisions

- **A budget is a scope, a period and a limit, kept on this computer per connection.** The scope is the server (every profile), a profile, a project (one profile's, by this computer's membership, ADR 0131) or a model (every profile). The period is a day, week or month on the clock of this computer's zone when the budget is made; the zone is kept with the budget, so a laptop that travels doesn't move its periods. Weeks start on Monday. The limit is in dollars or tokens.
- **What a budget measures depends on its scope, and the type says which.** A server, profile or project budget counts the chats started in this calendar day, week or month, from the stored totals Usage analytics already adds up (ADR 0026): Hermes counts a chat's whole cost when it started, and the tab says so. A model budget counts the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days of `GET /api/analytics/models`, which counts each call under the model that made it but only takes whole days back from now. A calendar figure for a model can't be had from it: its rolling figure also counts chats started before the period, and taking their chats' spending back out still leaves Hermes's helper tasks, which it folds into the same rows. A model budget's top chats are those that ended on the model, labelled so, since a chat row names only its last model.
- **What a budget spent carries how far it can be trusted.** Each figure is a lower bound, and lists every reason it is short or inexact: a profile that failed or reached the 5,000-chat read limit, compressed chats not read in full, tokens Hermes couldn't price (counted as $0, with the tokens shown and a token budget offered), a call priced only in part, Hermes's estimates, and for a model budget that its sums have no cache writes and include helper tasks and subagent runs. A budget with nothing to stand on (its only profile failed, the route missing, the project gone) shows no figure at all, never $0.
- **The forecast is the period's pace so far, or none.** What was spent, stretched over the whole period at the rate since it began. It is the only pace the figures support: a "last few hours" rate would miss an older chat that is still spending, since its cost counts at its start. It waits for 2 hours of a day, 12 of a week or 2 days of a month, and a model budget has none, since its stretch has no end.
- **One watch per live connection reads Hermes, and nothing else does.** A check reads every chat started since the earliest period any budget needs, once for all of them, and the per-model sums once per profile and day count a model budget needs. It runs every 5 minutes while connected, when the connection comes back, when a budget is saved, and when the tab opens (a check under 30 seconds old answers it). With no budgets it reads nothing. A compressed chat's segments other than its latest are finished, so each is read once per session. The tab shows the watch's board and asks it to save or remove; it holds no copy.
- **Each threshold is announced once per period and terms, and claimed before it is.** A budget keeps the highest threshold announced, with the period and the terms (period, zone, unit, limit) it was announced under. A mark of another period or other terms doesn't count, so a new period and an edited limit start again, and nothing has to reset it. A check announces at most one threshold per budget, the highest newly reached: one found at 85% says 80% once. The mark is written, after reading what is stored, before the notification goes out, so a second app window that checks the same budget finds it and stays quiet, and a crash between the two loses one notification rather than repeating it. A budget made or edited past a threshold is told at its next check: it's true. The notification names the budget's scope and figures, and never a chat.
- **Budgets are filed by profile.** A profile or project budget and its mark sit under the profile's scope of one `SCOPED_DOCS` document, `hermes-chat.budgets`, with no profile name inside; a server or model budget sits under the connection's. Renaming or deleting a profile on this computer, and removing the connection, carry or drop them with everything else (ADR 0156).

## Considered options

- **The tab reads Hermes, and a separate poller alerts.** Two readers, two copies of what was spent that can disagree in the same minute.
- **Calendar model budgets by each chat's last model.** The period would be exact, and the amount wrong for any chat that switched or failed over to another model.
- **A model budget's range: Hermes's rolling figure, less the spending of chats started before the period.** Not a true lower bound, since helper tasks in those chats stay in Hermes's rows.
- **Reading only the chats active since the last check (`order=recent`).** Hermes has the order, sorting by activity across a compressed chat's segments. A merge kept between checks has to follow a chat's id moving on compression and drop deleted chats, so it waits until a full read per check proves too costly.
- **Staying silent about a threshold already passed when a budget is made or a period starts.** Spending while the app was closed would never be announced.
- **A brake now.** Pausing cron jobs, goals and subagents comes in later PRs, each opt-in per budget and read back. The `Crossing` a check finds is where they hook in.

## Consequences

- Budgets are checked only while the app runs and is connected to the server; the tab says so.
- A check on a server with about 1,500 chats a month reads about 15 pages per profile every 5 minutes for a monthly budget.
- A chat's later spending counts in the period it started in, so a daily budget doesn't see yesterday's chat that is still running.
- Hermes's per-model sums count an unpriced model as $0 with no flag. A model row with tokens and no cost is treated as unpriced, which also catches a model included in a subscription.
